Pocketra Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

Pocketra helps you track your own money. We store the information you enter so the app can work on your account. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't share your financial information with anyone.

This policy explains what Pocketra ("the app", "we") collects, why, and what control you have. The app is published by [Your name].

What we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work:

  • Account details: your name and email address, and your password (handled by our authentication provider, which stores it in hashed form. We never see your password in plain text).
  • Financial entries you add: transactions (amount, category, note, date), budgets, recurring transactions, your monthly income, preferred currency, and timezone.
  • Profile photo (optional): only if you choose to upload one.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising ID, or any bank account or card numbers. Pocketra does not connect to your bank.

What stays on your device

  • Widget summary (if you add the home screen widget): a small snapshot of your balance, this month's spending, your fullest budget, and your next due recurring item, stored only on this device, so the widget has something to show the moment it's unlocked. It updates automatically as you use the app, and is cleared when you log out or delete your account.
    • Your PIN: stored only in your phone's secure storage. It is never sent to our servers.
    • Theme and notification preferences: saved locally on your phone.
    • Notifications: budget alerts and recurring reminders are created on your device. We don't send remote push notifications.

    How we use your information

    • To show your balance, transactions, budgets, charts, and reports.
    • To remind you about recurring items and budget limits, if you've allowed notifications.
    • To keep your account secure and let you sign back in.

    We don't use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to running the app.

    Where your data is stored

    Your account data is stored with Supabase, a database and authentication provider that processes it on our behalf. Data is sent over encrypted connections, and the database is set up so each account can only read and change its own records.

    When you export a CSV or PDF, the file is created on your device and shared only where you choose to send it.

    Who we share it with

    No one, apart from the service provider above that hosts the data for us. We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

    Your choices and deleting your data

    • You can edit your name, income, and profile photo in the app at any time.
    • You can delete individual transactions, budgets, and recurring items in the app.
    • To delete your account and all data tied to it, email pocketra.support@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.
    • You can turn notifications off in the app or in your phone's settings.

    Security

    We use industry-standard protections, including encrypted connections and per-account access rules. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. Choosing a PIN lock in the app adds protection if someone else uses your phone.

    Children

    Pocketra is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

    Changes to this policy

    If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and the effective date above. For significant changes we'll also tell you in the app.

    Contact

    Questions or requests about your data: pocketra.support@gmail.com
