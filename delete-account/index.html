Delete your Pocketra account

Published by Pocketra Team

You can delete your account and all of its data yourself, inside the app, in under a minute. Deletion is immediate and can't be undone.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Pocketra and go to the More tab.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

Want a copy of your records first? Use More → Export Data to save them as a CSV or PDF before you delete.

What gets deleted

  • The widget's saved balance summary on this device, if you added it
    • Your account and sign-in details
    • All transactions, budgets, and recurring items
    • Your profile information and profile photo
    • Your PIN and app preferences on the device

    We don't keep a copy of your financial entries after deletion.

    Can't open the app?

    If you can't sign in or have already uninstalled the app, email pocketra.support@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete your account within 30 days and confirm by reply.
